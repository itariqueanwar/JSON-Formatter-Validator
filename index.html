GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is this JSON formatter free?

Yes. It’s free to format, validate, and minify JSON. No account or subscription is required.

Do you upload or store my JSON?

No. Your input is parsed in your browser and is not sent to our servers.

What happens if my JSON is invalid?

The validator reports that the JSON could not be parsed and shows the browser’s syntax error so you can locate and fix the issue.

What does minify do?

Minifying removes indentation and other unnecessary whitespace. It preserves the data and structure.

Which JSON values are supported?

The tool uses your browser’s native JSON parser, which supports JSON objects, arrays, strings, numbers, booleans, and null.

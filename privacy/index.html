Privacy Policy

Your JSON stays private. JSON Formatter & Validator processes the data you enter on your device; it does not upload or store your JSON.

Your JSON data

When you format, validate, or minify JSON, your input is processed locally in your browser using its built-in JSON parser. It is not sent to Redsite or saved by this tool. The page does not retain your JSON after you close or reload the tab.

Information this site receives

The tool does not ask for an account, collect form submissions, or use advertising or analytics cookies. Like most websites, our hosting provider may process basic request and security data (such as IP address, browser details, and requested page) to deliver and protect the site.

Service providers

The page loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Your browser requests those font files directly from Google, which may receive standard connection data. JSON formatting and validation run locally in your browser.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact Redsite through redsite.in.

This policy applies to JSON Formatter & Validator at json.redsite.in.
